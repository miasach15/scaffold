Privacy Policy

Last updated August 23, 2026

Scaffold is built and run by one person, for a small number of people to use. This page explains what's collected and where it goes — plainly, since there isn't much to hide behind.

What's stored

Your account email, and whatever you enter into the app: calendar events, tasks, goals, habits, journal entries, education items and grades, and your settings (theme, categories, etc). It's stored in a Supabase project (Postgres database) with row-level security, so your data is only ever readable by your own signed-in account.

What's not collected

No ad trackers, no third-party analytics, no selling or sharing of your data with third parties for marketing. Nothing you write — no task, event, journal entry, goal, or anything else you type in — is ever read by anyone but you.

Basic usage tracking

Scaffold logs which top-level section you open (Calendar, Tasks, Goals, and so on) and when, so the app's own developer can see which parts of it actually get used. That's the entire extent of it — it does not log what you type, what you click on within a page, or any of your actual content, and it's stored in the same first-party database as everything else on this page, not sent to any outside analytics company.

Third-party services Scaffold uses

  • Supabase — hosts the database and handles sign-in/authentication.
  • Vercel — hosts and serves the app itself.
  • Anthropic (Claude API) — a few optional AI features (breaking a goal or assignment into steps) send the relevant text you typed to Claude to generate a response. This only happens when you use one of those specific features.

Each of these processes data under its own privacy terms as an infrastructure provider; none of them use your Scaffold data for their own purposes beyond providing the service.

Notifications

If you turn on push notifications, your browser/device generates a push subscription (no personal info beyond what's needed to deliver a notification to that device) which is stored so reminders can be sent. You can revoke this any time from your device or browser notification settings, or by turning it off in Scaffold.

Deleting your data

Email miasachdev15@gmail.com to have your account and all associated data permanently deleted.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the "last updated" date above will change too.
